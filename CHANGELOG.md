## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-20

### Changed

- The locked dependencies and the pre-commit hooks are updated by @danilogco
- The `kubescope-extension.toml` of this extension: the manifest says what is for production (`production = []`: only the manifest, `LICENSE`, `README.md` and `src`) by @danilogco

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-20

### Added

- The color themes of KubeScope that are not Light and Dark as an extension written against the SDK only: Abyss, Dark Modern, High Contrast, Light Modern, macOS Dark and Light, Midnight, Monokai, Quiet Light, Sepia, Solarized Dark and Light, and Tomorrow Night Blue
